Posted prices at the Levelland location. Bobtail prices apply to truck deliveries of up to 4,500 gallons; loads of 7,000–7,500 gallons are priced at the transport range below. Fuel prices are subject to change daily.
Transport prices shown are today’s indicative delivered prices for a 7,000–7,500-gallon transport load. Fuel is priced on the day it ships, not the day it is ordered. Orders placed early in the day can often ship the same day; otherwise, depending on order volume and market conditions, delivery may be one to two days out, and your invoice will reflect the posted price on the ship date, which may be higher or lower than shown here. Prices include freight and applicable taxes and fees, are updated each business day, and are subject to change without notice and to product availability.
